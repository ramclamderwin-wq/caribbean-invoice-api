# 3. Global customer acquisition strategy

**Owner direction:** Quillvora is a global platform. Interview locations are chosen for
learning speed and access, and they never limit the market. This document replaces all
earlier single-country recommendations.

## Who we acquire first (from `13-global-market-research.md`)

1. **Customs brokers:** highest willingness to pay, daily use, public licence lists.
2. **Small and mid-size freight forwarders that handle customs:** about 40,000 firms in
   FIATA's network; SMEs underserved by enterprise software.
3. **Mid-size importers:** later, through self-serve and search.

## Where we start acquiring (by opportunity and accessibility)

| Wave | Markets | Why | Lists and channels (facts where noted) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (now)** | **US, Canada, UK, Australia/NZ, Singapore** (English) | Largest willingness to pay; English supported; public registers | US: CBP broker data and NCBFAA; Canada: **CBSA publishes a licensed broker list**; UK: **HMRC publishes a list of customs agents**, and 80% of declarations go through intermediaries; associations (e.g. CSCB, BIFA, CBFCA); LinkedIn |
| **1 (now)** | **Mexico and Spanish-speaking Latin America** | Spanish already supported; fewer US-centric tools (assumption) | ~830–860 licensed *agentes aduanales* in Mexico (ANAM/padrón); LinkedIn; forwarder networks |
| **1 (opportunistic)** | **Caribbean** (via founder network) | Fast access to interviews and pilots (assumption) | Customs authority broker lists, personal network |
| 2 | EU (Germany, Netherlands, Spain, France, Italy, Poland) | Large and multilingual; needs AI engine and GDPR readiness | Associations, forwarder networks (WCA, FIATA) |
| 2 | Asia-Pacific beyond English markets (e.g. Philippines, Malaysia, India, Vietnam) | Largest brokerage revenue region (~42% in one report) | Partners and resellers |
| 3 | Middle East, Africa, other ASYCUDA countries | Growth markets; country packs by demand | Partners |

## How (channels by stage)

**Stage A: validation (weeks 1–6)**
- Outbound interviews by video call. **Quota: 20+ interviews in total, no more than 40%
  from one country, at least 3 countries, and both brokers and forwarders represented.**
- Sources:
  - public broker and agent registers;
  - associations;
  - LinkedIn searches ("customs broker", "licensed customs broker", "freight forwarder
    customs", "agente aduanal");
  - WCA/FIATA directories;
  - the founder's network.
- Offer to process 3–5 of their real invoices for free, to show results on their own documents.

**Stage B: concierge pilot and early revenue (weeks 7–12)**
- Paid pilots in any country (they run by email and spreadsheet).
- Collect testimonials and case studies from different countries to show global use.

**Stage C: scalable acquisition (month 3 onward)**
- **Self-serve:** website, free trial (e.g. 20 invoices), transparent USD pricing, card
  payment.
- **Search content** in English and Spanish first: "commercial invoice to Excel", "extract
  line items from invoice for customs", "packing list to spreadsheet".
- **A free single-invoice converter** as a lead magnet, with rate limits and abuse controls.
- **Partnerships:** forwarder networks (WCA-type), associations, and resellers in regions
  where we have no presence.
- **Integrations and marketplaces:** listings once connectors exist (accounting and
  forwarding software).
- **API program:** for software vendors once accuracy is proven.

## Messaging (global)

- "Commercial invoices to structured customs data, in minutes, reviewed and exportable,
  for brokers and forwarders worldwide."
- Show supported languages and currencies explicitly. Use case studies from several
  countries. Never position the product as regional.

## Metrics by stage

| Stage | Key metrics |
|---|---|
| A | Interviews by segment and country; pain score; quoted price acceptance; invoices received |
| B | Paid pilots; minutes saved per invoice; conversion to subscription; errors per 20 invoices |
| C | Trial sign-ups by country and channel; trial-to-paid %; customer acquisition cost; monthly churn; net revenue retention; revenue by region |

## Honest constraints

- **Language:** the rules engine reads English and Spanish only. Worldwide languages need
  the AI engine (benchmark approval pending).
- **Currencies:** limited to the current 22 by owner decision; expand when customers in
  other currencies sign up.
- **Privacy:** GDPR/UK GDPR readiness (DPA, sub-processors, possibly EU hosting) before an
  EU/UK sales push.
- **Competition:** strongest in the US. Win with self-serve, price, speed to value and
  review-first trust; track conversion by country.
