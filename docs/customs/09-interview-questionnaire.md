# 9. Customs broker / importer interview questionnaire

**Rules for the interviewer:**
- Ask about **past behaviour and real numbers**, not opinions about a hypothetical product.
- Do not mention RAMVORA, AI or extraction until section F.
- Let silences run. Ask "Can you show me?" and "When did that last happen?"
- 30–45 minutes. Record (with permission) or take verbatim notes.

## A. Context (5 min)
1. Tell me about your firm: what kinds of clients and goods, and how many people work on declarations?
2. Roughly how many entries/declarations do you prepare in a typical month? In your busiest month?
3. Walk me through what happens from the moment a client sends documents to when the entry is released.

## B. Current process (10 min)
4. Which documents do you receive, and how (email, WhatsApp, paper, portal)?
5. **Could you show me a recent commercial invoice (with confidential details hidden) and how you handled it?**
6. Who enters the invoice information into the system? How long does a typical invoice take? A long or difficult one?
7. What makes an invoice difficult? (Let them answer before giving examples.)
8. What tools do you use today for this step: the customs system directly, spreadsheets, any software?

## C. Problems and costs (10 min)
9. What are the three biggest problems in running your operation right now? *(Record the order verbatim, without prompting.)*
10. When did an invoice-related mistake last cause a problem? What happened, and what did it cost (time, fees, client)?
11. What happens in peak periods? How do you cope?
12. If you could change one thing about how documents reach you or get processed, what would it be?

## D. Past attempts (5 min)
13. Have you tried anything to speed up or simplify this work: software, outsourcing, hiring, templates? What happened?
14. *(If they used a tool)* Why did you keep it or stop using it? What did it cost?
15. How do you usually buy software or services? Who decides, and who pays?

## E. Money and priorities (5 min)
16. What do you charge clients per entry, roughly? *(Context for value, not to negotiate.)*
17. What does a data-entry clerk cost you per month, roughly?
18. Where does this problem rank compared with others you mentioned? Is anything budgeted to fix it?

## F. Solution reaction (only now; 5–10 min)
19. *Describe neutrally:* "Some firms upload supplier invoices and receive a spreadsheet of the invoice details and every line item, flagged for checking, which they review before entering." Would something like that be useful in your process? Where would it fit, and where wouldn't it?
20. What would it need to do for you to trust it?
21. What concerns would you have (confidentiality, accuracy, cost, staff)?
22. *Price test:* "A pilot for one month covering up to N invoices would cost US$X." What's your reaction? At what price would it be too expensive to consider? So cheap you'd doubt it?
23. Would you be willing to try a paid pilot on your real invoices next month? *(If yes, agree the next step and date; if no, ask what would need to be true.)*

## G. Close (2 min)
24. Could you share 3–5 recent invoices (redacted or with permission) so we can test on real documents? *(Use the consent form.)*
25. Who else should I speak to, among brokers or importers?

## After each interview (within 2 hours)
- Fill in the tracking sheet row (see `08-validation-programme.md`).
- Score the pain from 0–3:
  - 0 = not mentioned;
  - 1 = mentioned when prompted;
  - 2 = top-3 unprompted;
  - 3 = top-3 with a quantified cost.
- Note exact words used to describe the problem; they become marketing language later.

## Signals to watch
- **Strong:**
  - they show you their workaround;
  - they quantify the cost;
  - they ask when they can start;
  - they offer invoices without being asked.
- **Weak:** "Sounds interesting", "Send me information", compliments with no next step.
