# Outreach templates: English

**DRAFTS: do not send without owner approval.** Personalise every message (company
name, port or city, one specific detail from their website). Send from a business
address with a real name and signature, and include an opt-out line where the law
requires it (e.g. CAN-SPAM in the US, CASL in Canada, PECR/GDPR in the UK/EU). Never
attach files to a first message.

The goal of every first message is a **20–30 minute conversation about their process**,
not a sale.

---

## 1. LinkedIn connection request (≤ 300 characters)

> Hi [First name], I'm researching how customs brokers and forwarders handle supplier
> commercial invoices (keying line items, checking totals). Would you be open to sharing
> your experience in a short call? No sales pitch. — [Your name], Quillvora

## 2. First email (broker or forwarder)

**Subject:** Question about commercial invoice data entry at [Company]

> Hi [First name],
>
> I'm [Your name], founder of Quillvora. We're building software that turns supplier
> commercial invoices into structured line-item data for customs entries, and before we
> build more, I want to learn from people who do this work every day.
>
> Would you have 20–30 minutes in the next two weeks to tell me how [Company] handles
> commercial invoices today: how they arrive, who keys them, and what slows you down? I'm
> not selling anything on this call. If it's useful, I'm happy to run 3–5 of your
> invoices through our tool afterwards at no cost, with your permission.
>
> Would [two time options in their time zone] work?
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name] · Quillvora · [website] · [phone]
>
> *If you'd prefer not to hear from me again, just reply "no thanks".*

## 3. Follow-up (5–7 days later, once only)

**Subject:** Re: Question about commercial invoice data entry at [Company]

> Hi [First name], just bringing this back to the top of your inbox. If invoice data
> entry isn't a pain point for [Company], that's useful to know too; a one-line reply
> would help. Thanks, [Your name]

## 4. Referral or introduction request (from a contact or association)

> Hi [Name], I'm speaking with customs brokers and freight forwarders about how they
> process supplier invoices for customs entries. Is there someone in your network who
> handles that work and might share 20 minutes of their experience? I'd be grateful for
> an introduction.

## 5. Interview confirmation

**Subject:** Confirmed: [date/time, their time zone] call about invoice processing

> Thanks, [First name]. Confirming [date, time, time zone] on [video link].
> I'll ask about your current process; there's nothing to prepare. If you're comfortable,
> have a recent (redacted) commercial invoice handy to show me how you work with it.
> I won't record without asking first.

## 6. Thank-you and sample-invoice request (after the interview)

> Thank you for your time today, [First name]. Your point about [specific thing they
> said] was especially helpful.
>
> As mentioned, we'd be glad to process 3–5 of your recent commercial invoices to show
> you the result. Please only send them after signing the short consent form attached
> ([link]), which explains how we handle and delete the documents. Redacted copies are
> fine.

## 7. Pilot offer (only after the owner approves terms and price)

**Subject:** Proposal: 4-week Quillvora pilot for [Company]

> Hi [First name],
>
> Following our conversation and the results on your sample invoices, here's the pilot
> we discussed:
> - 4 weeks, up to [150] commercial invoices;
> - same-business-day turnaround;
> - every invoice checked by our team before delivery;
> - output in your preferred spreadsheet layout.
>
> The price is **US$[249]**, credited against your first [2] months if you continue.
> Quillvora prepares data only; your team remains responsible for classification and
> declarations.
>
> The pilot agreement is attached for your review. Would you like to start on [date]?

---

**Never say:**
- "99% accurate", or any accuracy figure;
- "automatic classification" or "we calculate duties";
- "AI does it all";
- any claim about clients we don't have.
