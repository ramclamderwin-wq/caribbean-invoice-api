# RAMVORA Acceptable Use Policy: DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW

> **Draft v1 (2026-11-26). Not legal advice.** Incorporated into the Terms of Service.

You may use RAMVORA only for lawful business purposes. You must not:

1. **Unauthorised documents:** upload documents you do not have the right to process, or that
   contain data you are not permitted to share with a service provider.
2. **Sensitive data:** deliberately upload health, biometric, government ID images, payment-card
   numbers or other special-category data not needed for invoice processing.
3. **Illegal activity:** use the Service for fraud, money laundering, sanctions evasion, trade-based
   fraud (for example mis-declaring goods or values), or any unlawful purpose.
4. **Security:** probe, scan or test the Service's vulnerabilities without written permission;
   access other customers' data; share, sell or publish API keys; or bypass authentication, rate
   limits or usage limits.
5. **Disruption:** overload the Service, send automated traffic designed to degrade it, or upload
   malicious files.
6. **Reverse engineering and competing use:** copy, scrape or reverse-engineer the Service, or use
   it to build a competing product, except as allowed by law.
7. **Resale:** resell or white-label the Service without a written agreement.
8. **Misrepresentation:** present RAMVORA output as verified, certified or official, or as customs,
   tax or legal advice.

**Enforcement:** we may warn, throttle, suspend or terminate accounts that violate this policy, and
report illegal activity where required by law.
**Report abuse:** support@ramvora.io.
